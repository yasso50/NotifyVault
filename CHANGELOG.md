# Changelog

All notable changes to NotifyVault are documented here.

## [1.0.0] - Production Prototype Release

### Added
- **Core Engine**: Full `NotificationListenerService` lifecycle handling with automatic deduplication.
- **Search & Timeline**: Instant sub-millisecond search across apps, titles, texts, and tags.
- **Privacy & OTP Guard**: Automatic detection and masking of verification codes in English and Arabic.
- **Material 3 Interface**: Android 15 design system with Light and Dark themes.
- **Bilingual i18n**: English and Arabic (RTL) localization.
- **Insights & Heatmap**: 7-Day × 24-Hour activity distribution and peak hour analytics.
- **Retention Worker**: Automated periodic cleanup worker via WorkManager.
- **Android Studio Project**: Complete native Kotlin codebase with Room database, Gradle build scripts, ProGuard rules, and JUnit tests.
